* Sharpe is the old app's: P/L summed by close date, mean/stdev times the square root of 252, with days that had no close not zero-filled. It ranks these rows against each other and is not comparable with a published Sharpe. TOTAL's ann ret % divides by the portfolio's PEAK DEPLOYED capital - the peak of the summed series, which is lower than the sum of the per-strategy peaks unless they all peak together - and its avg P/L % pools per-trade percentages, each against its own strategy's capital.
Phase 2 of 7 — allocation and filters
Strategies load, qty and capital allocate, the shared metric registry filters each one, and the summary table totals them. Every figure comes from the same code the single-backtest page uses, so a strategy reads the same on both.

Next: P3 equity, drawdown and capital deployed; P4 the correlation work, with both correlations weekly; P5 saved portfolio profiles; P6 distribution, overlap and rolling risk.

P/L is dated by close. Deployment is half-open — a position is capital at risk on the sessions it is held through and not on the one it closes on, so a weekly roll reads one position and not two on the day it hands over.
