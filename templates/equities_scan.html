{#- NOT `defer`, and that is the whole reason this page was dead. Alpine is loaded with `defer` in the head. Deferred scripts run in document order after parsing, so a deferred bundle HERE runs AFTER Alpine has started and dispatched `alpine:init` — the listener that registers the component is added to an event that has already fired, Alpine.data('equitiesScan') is never called, and every expression on the page throws "equitiesScan is not defined". A plain script at the end of the body executes DURING parsing, before any deferred script, so the component is registered before Alpine boots. That is why equities_live.html and equities_scalp.html have no `defer` here either, and it is now gated in check_rendered_assets.py rather than left as something to remember. -#}
